Coordinates are integers, so `clipper2` runs its 64 family, `pyclipr` runs with
`scaleFactor = 1.0` and `pyclipper` gets the values unscaled: all three see the
same integers. Fill rule is non-zero everywhere. Times are the best of 5 runs and
include, for every library, the conversion from the numpy input, the clipper
object's construction and the conversion of the result back — shapely included,
whose geometries are built from the same arrays inside the timed call.
`area` is the total area of the result and `(...)` its relative difference from
clipper2's.

## Offsetting with round joins

### 1000-vertex rosette, delta = 50000

Round joins are approximated differently by each library (clipper2 and pyclipr from `arc_tolerance`, pyclipper from Clipper1's `ArcTolerance`, shapely from `quad_segs`), so the areas differ by construction; the vertex count shows how finely each one drew the arcs.
